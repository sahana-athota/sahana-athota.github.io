## 🚀 Features

### 🏠 Homepage
A clean and responsive landing page introducing users to the brand’s essence, offering a quick glance at top trends, customer love, and service highlights.

### 🔄 Carousel Switch
Dynamic carousels showcasing trending outfits, new arrivals, and seasonal picks with smooth transitions and mobile responsiveness.

### 🃏 Cards
Stylish cards used across the site for:
- Clothing items with price & ratings
- Feature highlights
- User profiles and testimonials

### 📈 Graphs & Stats
Interactive visualizations representing:
- Comfort ratings across categories (T-Shirts, Joggers, etc.)
- Monthly fashion trend shifts
- Customer satisfaction & product popularity

### 💬 Testimonials
Real stories from real users — carousel-based testimonial section sharing their experience with our products.

### 🧑‍🤝‍🧑 Customers
A trust-building segment featuring brands, repeat customers, and loyalty statistics — designed to boost credibility.

### 🎯 Feature Services
Quick-view icons and descriptions for services like:
- Free Shipping
- 24/7 Customer Support
- Easy Returns
- Sustainable Fabrics
